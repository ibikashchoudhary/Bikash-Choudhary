My Approach

"Finance should drive decisions, not just document them."

Most financial reporting tells you what happened last month. I'm interested in what it means for next month — and how the numbers in front of leadership become the confidence behind their choices.

I've spent nine years building that kind of finance function at Inspiria Knowledge Campus: structured control frameworks, real-time compliance tracking, automated reporting, and payroll systems that simply work. Not flashy, but foundational.

I'm also genuinely excited about the role AI tools play in finance today — I've been using Claude and ChatGPT for agreement reviews, compliance support, and workflow design since before it became common practice.
