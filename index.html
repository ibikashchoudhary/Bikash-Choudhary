About Me

A journey built on curiosity, rigour, and steady growth.

I started my career at a reputed Chartered Accountant (CA) firm in Siliguri in December 2017 while completing my B.Com. In April 2018, I joined Inspiria Knowledge Campus, where I have continued to build my expertise in finance, accounting, compliance, payroll, ERP systems, MIS reporting, and process automation.

Over the past nine years, I have developed a strong foundation in financial management while continuously adopting technology to improve efficiency and decision-making.

Approach

"Finance should support decisions, not just document them."

Most financial reporting tells you what happened last month. I try to focus on what it means for next month — and how the numbers in front of leadership can become a useful basis for their choices.

Over the past nine years, I've worked on building that kind of finance function: structured control frameworks, compliance tracking, streamlined reporting, and payroll processes that run smoothly. Nothing flashy — just steady, foundational work.

I'm also genuinely interested in the role AI tools can play in finance — I've been using Claude and ChatGPT to help with agreement reviews, compliance support, and workflow design.
